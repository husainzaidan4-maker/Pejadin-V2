## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a dignified, high-efficiency digital operating standard tailored for official state bureaucracy, administrative compliance, and public sector accountability. Designed specifically for official duty travel management (Pengelolaan Perjalanan Dinas / PEJADIN), the interface instills institutional trust, rigorous financial precision, and clarity across multi-tiered approval workflows.

The design philosophy adopts a **Corporate / Modern Enterprise** aesthetic anchored in statecraft authority. It merges the structure of institutional governance with streamlined enterprise utility:
- **Tone:** Authoritative, disciplined, transparent, and legally binding.
- **Audience:** Civil servants (Aparatur Sipil Negara / ASN), budget authorization officers (Pejabat Pembuat Komitmen / PPK), internal compliance auditors (Inspektorat), and financial verifiers.
- **Visual Stance:** Ultra-crisp data layouts, structured metadata density, dignified official branding, and zero extraneous decorative elements. White space is functional and deliberate, ensuring swift document verification and seamless auditing against official expenditure standards (Standar Biaya Masukan / SBM).

## Colors

The palette balances bureaucratic prestige with strict functional status communication. Surfaces remain predominantly crisp and luminous, allowing dense audit data to remain readable during extended administrative shifts.

### Core Swatches
- **Primary Navy (`#0F2942`)**: Deep ministerial blue serving as the structural anchor. Applied to the primary navigation bar, active sidebar states, primary action buttons, key document headings, and official header banners.
- **Secondary Slate Blue (`#2563EB`)**: Technical link color, interactive tab indicators, active pagination, and sub-action triggers.
- **Tertiary Amber (`#D97706`)**: Verification queues, pending actions, and mid-level review warnings.
- **Neutral Slate (`#64748B`)**: Secondary typography, inactive icons, breadcrumbs, and structural framing.

### Structural & Surface Colors
- **Canvas Base (`#F8FAFC`)**: Soft cool off-white background preventing screen glare while separating high-elevation containers.
- **Card/Container Surface (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure white for tabular blocks, verification cards, form containers, and modal sheets.
- **Subtle Border (`#E2E8F0`)**: Low-contrast boundary lines providing geometric structure across tables, cards, and input fields.
- **Divider (`#CBD5E1`)**: Section division within formal document previews and signature chains.

### Official State Status Badges & Alerts
- **Draft (`Draft`)**: Surface `#F1F5F9`, Text `#475569`, Border `#CBD5E1`.
- **Submitted (`Diajukan`)**: Surface `#FEF3C7`, Text `#B45309`, Border `#FCD34D`.
- **Issued / Active (`Terbit`)**: Surface `#DBEAFE`, Text `#1D4ED8`, Border `#93C5FD`.
- **Completed (`Selesai`)**: Surface `#D1FAE5`, Text `#047857`, Border `#6EE7B7`.
- **Rejected / Cancelled (`Dibatalkan`)**: Surface `#FFE4E6`, Text `#BE123C`, Border `#FDA4AF`.
- **SBM Violation / Warning Highlighting**: Background `#FFF1F2`, Text `#9F1239`, Border `#FDA4AF`. Used exclusively for budget threshold breaches (Standar Biaya Masukan), duplicate SPPD schedules, or overdue accountability reports (LPJ).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy prioritizes systematic clarity, neutral legibility, and high numeric scannability across dense records and formal letters (Surat Tugas / SPPD).

- **Headlines & Section Titles:** Rendered in **Plus Jakarta Sans**, offering a polished, modern, yet strictly institutional Indonesian executive posture.
- **Body, Inputs, & Data Rows:** Handled by **Inter**, leveraging its tall x-height, clear differentiation of characters (e.g., `0`, `O`, `1`, `l`), and neutral cadence for financial grids and form fields.
- **Identifier Codes & Budget Account Codes (MAK/AKUN):** Utilize tabular numeric alignment or monospaced font tokens (`mono-code`) to guarantee strict vertical alignment of currency values (Rupiah), NIP (Nomor Induk Pegawai), and official document numbers.

## Layout & Spacing

The system implements a structured **Fluid Grid** architecture built around desktop enterprise productivity, maintaining an intentional split between navigation utility and audit workspace:

- **Desktop Layout:** Fixed vertical navigation sidebar (260px expanded, 72px condensed) paired with a persistent top administrative header (64px) housing institutional identity and notifications. Content canvas expands dynamically with a maximum bound of 1600px, utilizing a 12-column layout with `1.25rem` gutters.
- **Dense Data Zones:** Tabular datasets and complex multi-step application wizards adopt a 24-column subdivision or horizontal multi-panel split (e.g., Form Input 60% / Live Document & Budget SBM Validation 40%).
- **Mobile & Tablet Reflow:** Below 1024px, the sidebar transitions into an authoritative slide-over drawer; table columns prioritize Pegawai, Tujuan, and Status, while secondary financial breakdowns collapse into expandable disclosure rows. Canvas margins compress to `margin-sm` (`1rem`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on **Low-contrast outlines** paired with **Tonal layers**, avoiding excessive soft drop shadows that compromise official clarity.

- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Flat `#F8FAFC`. Base workspace for non-interactive layout backgrounds.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Blocks):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surface bounded by a crisp 1px solid border in `#E2E8F0`. No shadow is applied under standard state.
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns, Popovers, & Hover States):** `#FFFFFF` surface with border `#CBD5E1` and an understated ambient shadow: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.06), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Multi-step Workflow Dialogs):** `#FFFFFF` container with `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.04)` over a 40% `#0F2942` tinted backdrop.
- **Warning Overlays:** Surfaces flagging compliance violations use a tinted `#FFF1F2` layer with a direct `#FDA4AF` outline, visually commanding priority without relying on ambient elevation.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a **Soft (Level 1)** geometric standard. This creates an orderly, disciplined profile that preserves maximum usable screen real estate and reflects the seriousness of public sector records.

- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Badges):** 0.25rem (4px) border radius.
- **Medium Panels (Cards, Table Containers, Warning Banners):** 0.5rem (8px) border radius.
- **Large Contexts (Audit Drawers, Modals):** 0.75rem (12px) border radius.
- **Pills:** Forbidden for primary structural containers; reserved solely for numeric counter chips within navigation lists and tabular status indicators.

- **Right Cluster:** Active fiscal year selector badge (`TA 2025`), notification bell with pending approvals counter pill, and civil servant profile summary (Nama, NIP, Role).

- **Background:** `#FFFFFF` with 1px border-right `#E2E8F0`.
- **Navigation Items:** Height 40px, font `label-md`. Inactive state: `#475569`. Active state: `#0F2942` text with `#F1F5F9` background fill and a 3px vertical `#2563EB` left accent marker.

### Buttons
